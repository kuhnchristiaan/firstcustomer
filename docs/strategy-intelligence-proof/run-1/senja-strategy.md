## Know whether it is working

Collect: Distinguish replies from qualified interest: an owner confirms a current collection problem and agrees to evaluate Senja. Activation means obtaining a real testimonial or publishing existing proof; adoption requires subsequent use. Neither establishes payment.

Continue: Provisionally continue if two owners express qualified interest and one activates; investigate whether the verified paid offer solves a remaining need.

Adjust: Change messaging or onboarding when replies reveal relevance but unclear value or activation friction.

Reconsider: Reconsider targeting if conversations repeatedly show no timely need; reconsider access if free contact routes are unavailable. Silence after one week is inconclusive.
