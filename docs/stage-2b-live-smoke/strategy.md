# Senja — genuine signed-in advisor strategy

Original GPT-6.1 Sol output, unchanged. Context: launched with no paying customers; 3 hours/week; USD 0/month; no previous acquisition efforts.

## Target buyer

Bootstrapped SaaS founders with happy paying users who are preparing a launch or sales-page refresh.

They need customer proof for an upcoming selling moment and can authorize a software purchase themselves.

Existing customers make testimonial collection actionable. This narrower buyer is preferable to pre-revenue founders without customer evidence.

## Acquisition channel

Permission-based participation in Indie Hackers discussions

Find recent founder milestone and launch posts; contribute relevant public replies and introduce Senja only where permitted and useful.

Posts can reveal an immediate trigger before contact, unlike broad cold outreach. With no previous acquisition efforts, no paying customers, and no budget, this tests problem relevance without building a prospect database.

### Check access

Read current community rules and search recent posts for 'first customers', 'launch', and 'testimonials'. Identify three suitable founders and confirm a free, permitted reply route before preparing promotion.

### Conditional fallback

If access fails, check r/SaaS for a current, explicitly permitted feedback or promotion thread serving the same buyer. Proceed only if suitable founders and posting permission are evident; otherwise pause publication.

## First seven days

### 1. Validate access and offer

Perform the access check and inspect Senja's actual plans, paid capabilities, and purchase path. Do not propose payment until the offer is verified.

Record: Suitable discussions, applicable rules, and verified paid offer.

30 minutes · USD 0

### 2. Prepare a useful demonstration

Prepare one short walkthrough showing how a founder could create a collection form and gather a customer testimonial. Use sample content clearly marked as such.

Record: Walkthrough and any activation friction.

20 minutes · USD 0

### 3. Participate selectively

Review up to ten recent discussions and make up to five individually relevant replies where rules allow. Ask about their collection obstacle; offer the walkthrough without promising sales gains.

Record: Posts reviewed, buyer fit, replies published, and responses.

45 minutes · USD 0

### 4. Support interested founders

Answer responses. Invite interested founders to collect one real testimonial in Senja; discuss the verified paid offer only when it meets their stated need.

Record: Buying needs, activation, objections, and checkout outcomes.

35 minutes · USD 0

### 5. Make the next decision

Review evidence after seven days and choose whether to repeat, revise, or pause.

Record: Separate engagement, usage, and revenue evidence.

20 minutes · USD 0

## Resources

180 available minutes · USD 0 experiment budget.

Founder supplied three hours weekly and zero monthly budget. Tasks allocate 150 minutes, reserving 30 for interruptions and follow-up.

## Success criteria

Collect: Record replies separately from qualified interest: an authorized founder with a current need considering Senja. Activation is collecting a real testimonial; adoption requires repeated use. Only completed payment confirms revenue.

Continue: Provisionally continue if two qualified conversations emerge and one founder activates; test the verified paid offer where appropriate.

Adjust: Relevant replies without activation warrant examining onboarding or offer fit; insufficient permitted exposure warrants changing execution.

Reconsider: Reconsider if suitable buyers cannot be reached permissibly or qualified conversations repeatedly reveal no collection need. Silence or one week alone does not establish absent demand.

## Important unknowns

- Actual pricing and paid value relative to the buyer's need.

- Whether active, relevant discussions permit product participation.
